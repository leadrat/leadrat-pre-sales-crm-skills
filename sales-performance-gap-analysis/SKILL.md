# Sales Performance & Gap Analysis

## When to use this skill
Evaluate overall sales performance vs benchmark across the full funnel: Lead → Qualified Lead → Meeting → Site Visit → Booking. Use when the team needs to understand where performance is strong or weak, what the biggest gap is, and where to focus effort.

## What to analyze
- Conversion at each funnel stage (Lead → Qualified Lead → Meeting → Site Visit → Booking)
- Bookings and revenue totals
- Current period vs previous period comparison
- Agent contribution (individual and team)
- Project-wise contribution
- Source contribution
- Best-performing vs average vs underperforming segments

## What to identify
- Where overall performance is strong or weak
- The biggest performance gap vs benchmark
- Root cause of the gap
- Which segment has the highest business impact
- Recommended focus area

## Output structure
Performance → Gap → Reason → Impact → Recommended focus

## Example prompt
"Generate a sales performance & Gap report for the selected period"
