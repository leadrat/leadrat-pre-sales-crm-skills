# LeadRat MCP Prompt Library

A reference library of 160 drill-down prompts organised by category for deeper LeadRat MCP analysis. These prompts complement the nine dedicated analysis skills and cover daily operational queries through to strategic management reporting.

## Categories

1. **Daily Sales Performance** — Quick daily snapshots of team activity and pipeline health
2. **Sales Performance & Gap Analysis** — Deep-dive into funnel conversion and benchmark gaps
3. **Agent Performance & Productivity** — Individual agent activity, conversion, and coaching signals
4. **Lead Funnel & Conversion** — Stage-wise movement, velocity, and drop-off analysis
5. **Follow-up & Execution** — Activity compliance, response times, and missed opportunity audit
6. **Marketing Performance** — Channel effectiveness, campaign ROI, and source quality
7. **Project & Property Performance** — Project-level demand, conversion, and revenue contribution
8. **Project vs Marketing Fit** — Source-project alignment and lead quality per project
9. **Revenue Performance** — Revenue drivers, trends, and growth opportunities
10. **Lost Opportunity Analysis** — Lost and dead lead patterns, reasons, and recovery potential
11. **Management Action & Improvement** — Prioritised action plans and strategic recommendations
