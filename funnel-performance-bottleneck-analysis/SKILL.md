# Funnel Performance & Bottleneck Analysis

## When to use this skill
Identify where leads get stuck or lost in the funnel. Use when the team needs to pinpoint bottlenecks, quantify the opportunity cost of each choke point, and determine which agents, projects, or sources contribute most to funnel stalls.

## What to analyze
- New → Callback transition rate and waiting time
- Callback → Meeting transition rate and waiting time
- Meeting → Site Visit transition rate and waiting time
- Site Visit → Booking transition rate and waiting time
- Stalled leads at each stage
- Overall drop-off volumes per stage

## What to identify
- The biggest single bottleneck in the funnel
- The stage with the longest average waiting time
- The stage causing the biggest opportunity loss
- Contributing agents, projects, and sources to bottlenecks

## Output structure
Funnel Stage → Drop-off → Bottleneck → Impact → Focus Area

## Example prompt
"Generate a funnel performance report showing stage-wise conversion, bottlenecks, drop-offs, and areas needing attention."
