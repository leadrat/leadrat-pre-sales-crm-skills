# Revenue Performance & Gap Analysis

## When to use this skill
Understand revenue drivers, decline areas, and improvement opportunities. Use when leadership needs a clear picture of what is generating revenue, where revenue is at risk, and where the highest-value growth opportunities exist.

## What to analyze
- Total revenue and booking counts
- Revenue by project
- Revenue by source
- Revenue by agent
- Conversion rates contributing to revenue
- Average booking value
- Revenue trends over time (period-over-period)

## What to identify
- Top revenue drivers (project, source, agent)
- Revenue concentration risks (over-dependence on a single driver)
- High-value opportunities not yet converted
- Decline areas where revenue is falling
- Gaps between bookings volume and revenue value

## Output structure
Revenue Performance → Drivers → Gaps → Impact → Opportunity

## Example prompt
"Generate a revenue performance & gap report for the selected period, showing revenue drivers, gaps, trends, and areas with growth opportunities."
