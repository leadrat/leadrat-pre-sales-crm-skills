# Management Action & Improvement Plan

## When to use this skill
Turn multi-dimensional performance insights into a concrete, prioritised action plan. Use when leadership needs to move from analysis to decision — identifying quick wins, high-impact fixes, and long-term growth opportunities.

## What to analyze
- Sales performance gaps
- Marketing performance gaps
- Team/agent performance gaps
- Conversion and funnel gaps
- Project-specific gaps
- Lost or missed opportunities
- Business risks from current trajectory
- Growth opportunities

## What to identify
- The highest-impact problems requiring immediate action
- Quick wins achievable in the short term
- Medium-term improvement areas
- Long-term strategic opportunities
- Overall priority order across all dimensions

## Output structure
Problem → Evidence → Priority → Action → Expected Impact

## Example prompt
"Generate a sales improvement action plan based on my performance, marketing, team, funnel, project, and revenue data."
