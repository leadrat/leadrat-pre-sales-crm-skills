# Follow-up & Sales Execution Gap Analysis

## When to use this skill
Understand whether the sales team is properly working all available opportunities. Use when management needs to audit sales activity, identify untouched leads, and expose execution gaps before they become lost deals.

## What to analyze
- Leads with no activity recorded
- First response time per lead and per agent
- Follow-up count and recency per lead
- Overdue follow-ups
- Calls made and connected calls
- Recent activity across the team
- Stage progression vs time elapsed

## What to identify
- Untouched or neglected opportunities
- Follow-up gaps (leads not followed up within SLA)
- First-response gaps
- Activity vs conversion gaps (high activity, low conversion)
- Sales execution issues by agent

## Output structure
Sales Activity → Execution Gap → Missed Opportunity → Impact → Focus Area

## Example prompt
"Generate a sales execution report showing follow-up gaps, missed opportunities, response gaps, and areas needing attention."
