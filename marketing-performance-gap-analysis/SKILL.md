# Marketing Performance & Gap Analysis

## When to use this skill
Understand which sources and campaigns generate quality leads and business. Use when the team needs a clear view of marketing channel performance, period-over-period trends, and the marketing gaps affecting revenue.

## What to analyze
- Leads by source and campaign
- Qualified leads per source/campaign
- Conversion rates per source/campaign
- Site visits driven by each source/campaign
- Bookings and revenue attributed to each source/campaign
- Period-over-period comparison

## What to identify
- Best-performing marketing sources
- Poor-performing marketing sources
- High-volume, low-conversion sources wasting budget
- Declining campaigns
- Marketing gaps directly affecting revenue

## Output structure
Marketing Performance → Best/Weak Channels → Gap → Business Impact → Focus Area

## Example prompt
"Generate a marketing performance & gap dashboard for the selected Period"
